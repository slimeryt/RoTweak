What RoTweak does

RoTweak runs only on roblox.com in your browser. It adds an in-page settings drawer, optional reimagined pages (profile, inventory, friends, transactions), server region tools, a command palette, and performance/privacy tweaks — all toggled from settings.

First run: accept the agreement, then connect your Roblox account via OAuth. Settings live in chrome.storage on your device; tokens stay local until you disconnect or clear storage.

Where to configure

  • On Roblox — click the RoTweak button in the site header.
  • Popup — master enable, connect/disconnect, quick access.
  • Options — full settings page with search and account privacy.
  • Command palette — Ctrl+K / ⌘K when enabled.
