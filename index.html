RoTweak help

RoTweak upgrades the Roblox website in your browser — better servers info, cleaner pages, reimagined profile & inventory, chat, and more. Unofficial fan project; not affiliated with Roblox Corporation.

What you get

  • Smarter play & servers — region labels, uptime, filters, and optional auto-failover when joining.
  • Reimagined pages — optional new layouts for profile, inventory, friends, transactions, and avatar.
  • Less clutter — hide sponsored tiles, trim home noise, block trackers and heavy embeds.
  • Power tools — command palette (Ctrl+K), website chat, streamer mode, and more.

RoTweak only changes roblox.com in your browser. It does not modify Roblox games or clients. Most features are off by default — turn on what you want in the RoTweak panel or options.

Quick access

  • On Roblox — RoTweak button in the site header opens the settings panel.
  • Extension icon — quick on/off and link to full options.
  • Options — every setting, Roblox connection, and account privacy controls.
