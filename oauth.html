Connect Roblox

Optional sign-in that lets RoTweak know who you are and use Roblox’s official APIs on your behalf.

Why connect?

Many RoTweak features talk to Roblox while you browse — loading inventory, friends, avatar items, transactions, or your account privacy settings. Connecting is the safe way to do that without typing your password into the extension.

Roblox opens a normal login window. RoTweak never sees your password — only tokens stored locally on your device.

How to connect

  1. Open Roblox or click the RoTweak extension icon.
  2. Click Connect Roblox (or Connect in the first-run panel).
  3. Sign in on Roblox’s page if asked, and approve access.
  4. Return to Roblox — connected features will start working.

What RoTweak can access

Connection uses Roblox Open Cloud with basic profile scopes. RoTweak uses this to identify you and call APIs needed for the features you turn on. It does not run a separate server that collects your data — see the Privacy Policy.

How to disconnect

  • Popup or options — click Disconnect.
  • Clear storage in options — removes tokens and all settings.
  • Roblox account — revoke RoTweak in your Roblox connected apps / OAuth settings.
  • Uninstall the extension — removes everything from your browser.

If connect fails, wait a moment and try again. Make sure pop-ups aren’t blocked for Roblox. If you use multiple browser profiles, connect in the same profile where RoTweak is installed.

Do I have to connect?

Some tweaks (like hiding sponsored content or showing server regions on public lists) work without connecting. Anything that needs your inventory, friends, avatar, or private account data requires a connection.
