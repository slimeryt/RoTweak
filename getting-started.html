Getting started

After RoTweak is installed, open any Roblox page and follow these steps.

1. Open Roblox in your browser

Go to roblox.com and sign in as usual. RoTweak activates only on Roblox pages — not on other sites.

2. Accept the agreement

The first time RoTweak runs, a panel explains what the extension does. Read it and check the box to continue. If you decline, RoTweak stays off until you accept.

3. Connect Roblox (recommended)

Click Connect Roblox and sign in when Roblox asks. This links your account so features like inventory, friends, and API-backed tools work. See Connect Roblox for details on what that means.

4. Open the RoTweak panel

Click the RoTweak button in the Roblox header (top of the page). The panel slides in with categories — General, Gameplay, Servers, Social, and others.

  • Flip toggles to turn features on or off.
  • Changes save automatically and apply to open Roblox tabs.
  • Use the search box at the top to find a setting quickly.

5. Pick what you want

Nothing is forced on you. Start with one or two features — for example Show server region on game servers, or Reimagined profile on user profiles — then explore all features when you're ready.

Master switch: turn off Enable RoTweak to disable everything at once without losing your preferences. You can also use the extension popup icon for a quick off switch.

Something not working?

  • Refresh the Roblox tab after changing settings.
  • Make sure Enable RoTweak is on and you're connected if the feature needs it.
  • Try disconnecting and reconnecting Roblox from options.
  • After a Roblox site update, some features may need an extension update — check for a new version.
