RoTweak Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

RoTweak is a browser extension that improves the Roblox website experience in your browser. It is an unofficial fan project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Roblox Corporation.

What we collect

RoTweak does not operate a backend that collects personal data for advertising or resale.

  • Settings & preferences are stored locally in your browser (chrome.storage) on your device.
  • Roblox OAuth tokens (access/refresh) are stored locally in extension storage after you choose Connect Roblox. They are used only to verify your Roblox identity and to call Roblox Open Cloud APIs you authorize.
  • When features call Roblox or related APIs (for example region lookups), those requests go from your browser to those services under your session or OAuth grant.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not require an RoTweak account.
  • We do not put your Roblox password in the extension.
  • We do not ship a client secret or API key for your account inside the extension package beyond the public OAuth Client ID.

Third parties

Authorization and Open Cloud access are handled by Roblox under their own policies. Optional helper services used by some features (for example IP/region helpers) are contacted only when those features run.

Your choices

  • Decline the first-run agreement to keep features off.
  • Disconnect Roblox from the popup or options page to revoke local tokens.
  • Use Clear storage in options to wipe settings, agreement, and tokens.
  • Uninstall the extension to remove it from your browser.

Children

RoTweak is intended for users who already use Roblox in a browser. If you are under the age required by Roblox or your local law to consent to third-party apps, ask a parent or guardian before connecting.

Changes

We may update this policy when features change. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.

Contact

For privacy questions about RoTweak, open an issue on the project repository or contact the maintainer who distributed the extension to you.
